Zambia · Payroll Workbook

Capital: Lusaka · Currency: Zambian kwacha (ZMW)

Country coverage status

Zambia has been added as country 108 in the SuccessLabs atlas. SAP Employee Central Payroll localized payroll country-version support is not verified in the pinned 2H 2026 country-specific guide. Do not assume statutory localization or run payroll from this educational page.

Learning pathway

  1. Confirm legal employer, worker scope and pay frequency.
  2. Research current Zambia Revenue Authority and NAPSA obligations from official sources.
  3. Validate product support, local partner requirements and supported SAP release.
  4. Design employee master data, earnings, deductions, tax and contributions.
  5. Model pay period, bank payment, reporting, posting and controls.
  6. Test with synthetic employees and retain dated evidence.

Important

This is a clearly labelled initial country page, not yet a 108-step Zambia workbook. Country-specific configuration and statutory parameters require further research and verification.
