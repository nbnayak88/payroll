# Country coverage audit · 8 November 2026

The catalogue was compared with SAP’s [Country/Region-Specifics for Employee Central Payroll, 2H 2026](https://help.sap.com/doc/b24aa110d63d4602830fc57424142556/2611/en-US/SF_ECPAY_CountrySpec.pdf), document date 16 October 2026. Country entries were checked at chapter and appendix level. This is a document-coverage audit, not a statutory-localization certification.

| Collection | Resources | Learning scope |
|---|---:|---|
| Original country workbooks | 62 | Existing refreshed country learning workbooks |
| New main-chapter workbooks | 3 | Mexico, South Africa, United Kingdom; 48 design and learning sections each |
| New appendix editions | 42 | 12 guided replication experiments each; statutory calculations excluded |
| UK Payroll Configurator | 1 | Existing interactive companion |
| Total | 108 | 107 countries and regions |

All 54 main country chapters and 49 appendix country entries now have a workbook. Bahrain, Bolivia, Guyana and Paraguay are retained as four additional existing country assets. Seven appendix countries already had workbooks: Denmark, Ecuador, Iraq, Peru, Sri Lanka, Suriname and Uruguay.

UAE was found and refreshed at its existing URL; Uruguay and Venezuela completed the original 62-country refresh. The UK configurator is retained alongside the new UK workbook.

The new activities are original SuccessLabs learning material. The guide links identify the documented scope and source location. Main-chapter editions include country-specific authority links and design prompts; they do not preload unverified numerical tax parameters or represent completed tenant tests. Appendix editions focus on the cited replication entry and cannot establish full statutory payroll support.
